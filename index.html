How much color

Give the accent room to matter.

Let clear space and readable type carry the page. Treat the proportions as a visual guide, not a rigid formula.

Neutral canvas first

Use the light or dark canvas across most of the screen. It keeps long sessions comfortable.

Orange marks action

Reserve saturated orange for the primary action, progress, or one focal brand moment.

Yellow is a cue

Use yellow sparingly for a highlight or callout. It should not compete with orange.

Accessibility

Pair colors by role, not by habit.

Bright orange is a light color. It works well as a fill, but it is too light for small text on white and white text does not read clearly on it.

Keep these functional colors distinct from the brand palette. Pair each status color with a clear label or icon.
